Direction / Technical thesis

Production intent.
Executable contracts.

My thesis: industrial intelligence needs a representation that connects an intended physical outcome to machine actions and independently observed evidence.

The unit of executiontask = outcome + constraints + preconditions + actions + acceptance

A task is complete when its acceptance conditions are observed. A command acknowledgement is only one event in that process.

01

Represent the physical outcome.

Define target state, frame, tolerances and observations. Compile a process graph with preconditions and effects. Unknown units, geometry or capabilities block execution.

02

Resolve action against machine state.

Bind each step to an available resource and valid mode. Separate planning, supervision and servo timescales. Make final motion authority explicit across learned and classical controllers.

03

Use prediction where it changes action.

Use learned dynamics to choose actions, estimate contact or evaluate recovery. Measure rollout error and decision quality under hidden state and distribution shift.

04

Recover without losing the contract.

Preserve completed effects and remaining capabilities after divergence. Resume from verified preconditions; invalidate dependent steps when physical state changes.

05

Carry evidence into the next deployment.

Bind results to observations, timing, model identity and machine configuration. Measure adaptation effort and task success on a changed cell to establish transfer.

Evaluation questions

Can the task transfer?

Hold the outcome fixed, change a machine or fixture, then measure mapping effort, recovery and acceptance.

Can failure be detected?

Introduce stale state, interrupted motion or a missing observation. Check whether execution stops, recovers or withholds a verdict.

Does learning improve decisions?

Compare learned and classical choices under the same task constraints, including tail latency and failure cases.
